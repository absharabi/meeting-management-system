Transcript — 71st Board of Governors Meeting, NIT Calicut 11 December 2024, 4:00 PM |
Board Office, Administrative Block A
Chairperson (Prof. Prasad Krishna): Good afternoon everyone. I see we have quorum. Let's get
started. Registrar, please confirm.
Registrar (Cdr. Shamasundara M.S.): Yes sir, quorum is complete. Members joining via video
conference have also checked in.
Chairperson: Good. First item — confirmation of minutes from the 70th meeting. Members
received the draft in October. Any comments?
Dr. George Varghese: No objections from my side. The draft accurately reflects what was
discussed.
Prof. Sajith A.S.: Agreed.
Chairperson: Good, minutes confirmed. Registrar, take us through the action taken report
quickly.
Registrar: Most items are implemented or in progress sir. The delegation of financial powers
has been issued, honorarium orders for PhD examiners are out, the 20th convocation went
ahead on 7th September with 1969 graduands. CPWD projects are progressing though slightly
behind schedule — they've committed to March 2025 completion. Solar panel tender has been
floated through SECI. The SBI license fee matter, a letter has been sent to the bank as directed.
Shri. Sanjog Kapoor (via VC): On the CPWD delay — is March 2025 realistic?
Registrar: CPWD has assured us sir. We're monitoring closely. The reconciliation of accounts
with CPWD is also in its final stage.
Chairperson: Alright. Let's move to the Finance Committee minutes from today's earlier session.
Any items members want to revisit?
Prof. Asokan Thondiyath (via VC): The land lease proposal — the Cyber Park parcel. I want to
flag that 4.27 crores per acre is on the higher side. Has the institute negotiated?
Chairperson: That's exactly why we're giving in-principle approval only. The team will negotiate
both the rate and the lease period before anything is signed. The urgency is because the Centre
of Excellence in Urban Planning has already been awarded to us by the Ministry of Housing for
25 years and we need a base.
Smt. Veena Dunga (via VC): Since this wasn't in the circulated agenda, I'd suggest a detailed
letter to the Ministry before proceeding further.
Chairperson: Absolutely, that's been noted. FC has already recommended the same. Approved
in principle, letter to Ministry, negotiate favourably. Next — the International Hall of Residence,
PPP model.
Dr. M.G. Suresh Kumar: Good initiative. Which model are we following?
Chairperson: We're looking at the IIT models. The BoG instruction is to follow Government of
India PPP guidelines strictly. Approved in principle.
Chairperson: Now faculty reservation rosters and recruitment. We have a special drive proposed
for Pay Level 10 — 20 posts, with SC, ST, OBC breakdown — and concurrent internal
recruitment to Pay Level 11.
Prof. Sajith A.S.: The roster has been prepared with the Equal Opportunity Cell. Process looks
sound.
Chairperson: Any objections? No. Approved. Registrar, initiate the process.
Chairperson: Finance Committee nominee — with Shri Raveendran Kasturi's term having ended,
we need a new Board nominee to FC. I'm proposing Prof. Asokan Thondiyath.
Shri. V. Noushad (via VC): Seconded.
Chairperson: Approved. Prof. Thondiyath, welcome to the Finance Committee.
Prof. Asokan Thondiyath (via VC): Thank you, happy to contribute.
Chairperson: New programmes. Senate has sent us four proposals for regular programmes —
Mathematics and Computing dual degree, Energy Engineering B.Tech, BBA in Healthcare
Management, and the Integrated BS-MS in the science departments. These are substantial
proposals. What's the Board's view?
Shri. Sanjog Kapoor (via VC): These all require new faculty positions and infrastructure. Has the
financial implication been worked out?
Chairperson: That's precisely the point. We want to send a consolidated proposal to MoE, which
will go to the Finance Ministry for approval of new positions and funding. The reason for
urgency is if we want any chance of admitting students from 2025-26, the proposal needs to go
now.
Smt. Veena Dunga (via VC): Understood. Please send it at the earliest and we'll process it on
our end.
Chairperson: Agreed. Self-financing programmes — EMBA, EMTech, and the two certificate
programmes from CoELSCM — these are simpler since they don't need new government
funding.
Dr. George Varghese: The fee for EMBA at 12,500 per credit — has that been benchmarked?
Chairperson: Senate has suggested comparing with IIM fee structures. For the certificate
programmes, the CoELSCM chair will take separate approval on fees after that comparison.
Dr. George Varghese: Fine with me then.
Chairperson: All four self-financing programmes approved. Revenue sharing model — 30 to
IRG, 10 to department, 10 to administration, 50 to the faculty and coordinators. Applicable only
to self-financing courses, not government subsidised ones.
Prof. Sajith A.S.: That's a clear and fair split.
Chairperson: Approved. Offshore campus exploration — UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Saudi
Arabia.
Shri. Sanjog Kapoor (via VC): The institute must get MoE approval before reaching out to any
foreign country directly. Please don't initiate contact without that clearance.
Chairperson: Noted and accepted. We'll also study the IIT Delhi and IIT Madras models before
deciding our approach. Two faculty resignations for ratification — Dr. Suni Vasudevan and Dr.
Anudeep V. Ratified.
Chairperson: Disciplinary matters — the charge sheet for Dr. Muhammed Shafi. There's a
second charge sheet also being prepared. The Board's direction is that the draft must be
circulated to all BoG members for suggestions before it is issued.
Registrar: Understood sir.
Chairperson: 61 court cases pending. I'll repeat what I've said before — we need to work on
reducing this number. Some of these go back to 2005. Legal cell should be reviewing each case
for settlement possibilities.
Registrar: We'll do a review sir.
Chairperson: Pay fixation matter for the Registrar's post — referred to MoE. Ratified. Last item
— Director's proposal to delegate routine administrative matters to Deans and Registrar.
Dr. M.G. Suresh Kumar: This makes sense. The Director should be focused on strategic matters.
Chairperson: Approved. Financial delegation powers remain as previously decided by the Board,
only routine administrative approvals are being delegated. Any other business? No? Then we
close. Thank you all for your time. Meeting concluded at 5:30 PM.
Recorded by the Office of the Registrar, NIT Calicut
